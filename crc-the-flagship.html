Project Overview

CRC The Flagship is a landmark commercial development located at Plot 1B in Sector 140A, Noida. Spread across an 11-acre pre-certified IGBC Platinum rated green campus, the project offers Grade-A office spaces, business suites, serviced suites, and premium high-street retail stores. Designed in partnership with world-renowned master planners Benoy (UK), it features cutting-edge structural architecture, premium specifications, lush outdoor landscapes, and stellar connectivity via the Noida-Greater Noida Expressway.
