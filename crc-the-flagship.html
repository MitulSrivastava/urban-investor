Deep Dive Investment Guide

Everything you need to know about CRC The Flagship

CRC The Flagship represents CRC Group's flagship commercial development in one of Noida's most premium commercial destinations—Sector 140A. Spread across 11 acres, this Grade-A commercial campus introduces a comprehensive mix of office spaces, retail shops, and luxury serviced suites, catering to diverse business and investment objectives.

The project's IGBC Platinum pre-certification ensures world-class sustainable features including energy-efficient systems, water conservation, and indoor environmental quality. These certifications are increasingly important for attracting premium corporate tenants who prioritize sustainability.

For investors, CRC The Flagship offers multiple investment options starting from ₹72.45 Lakhs, making Grade-A commercial space accessible. The serviced suites are particularly attractive for investors seeking regular rental income, while commercial offices and retail shops offer capital appreciation and rental yields.

CRC The Flagship is designed as a comprehensive commercial destination covering 11 acres. The 11-acre campus is meticulously planned with dedicated zones for offices, retail, and serviced suites, ensuring efficient operations and professional environment across uses.

Grade-A office spaces feature modern floor plates designed for IT/ITES companies, corporate headquarters, and professional service firms. The offices come with efficient layouts, premium finishes, and modern building services including high-speed elevators, 100% power backup, and fiber-optic connectivity.

High-street retail shops face the main road, ensuring maximum visibility and footfall for businesses. These units are ideal for banks, food & beverage outlets, pharmacies, and services catering to the office population.

The luxury serviced suites offer fully furnished, hotel-managed accommodation units that generate rental income through professional management. These suites appeal to business travelers, medical tourists, and professionals seeking quality short-to-medium-term stays.

CRC The Flagship introduces comprehensive commercial features:

  • 11-Acre Campus: Expansive commercial campus with dedicated zones for offices, retail, and serviced suites in a professionally designed environment.
  • IGBC Platinum Pre-Certified: Highest green building certification ensuring sustainability, efficiency, and premium tenant attraction.
  • Multiple Products: Grade-A offices, high-street retail shops, and luxury serviced suites starting from ₹72.45 Lakhs.
  • Premium Amenities: Business center, conference facilities, food court, cafeteria, and visitors' parking creating comprehensive business ecosystem.
  • Modern Infrastructure: High-speed elevators, 100% power backup, fiber-optic connectivity, efficient HVAC, and multi-level parking.
  • Anchor Location: Sector 140A is emerging as a premium commercial node on the expressway with excellent connectivity and corporate ecosystem.

CRC The Flagship offers diverse commercial configurations:

Serviced Suites: Fully furnished units starting from ₹72.45 Lakhs*, professionally managed for rental income. Ideal for investors seeking regular returns without active management. These units target business travelers, medical tourists, and professionals.

Retail Shops: High-street retail units ranging from 300-1,000 sq.ft. with main road frontage. Perfect for banks, restaurants, cafes, pharmacies, and services catering to office-goers.

Office Spaces: Grade-A offices ranging from 500-5,000+ sq.ft. designed for IT/ITES, corporate offices, and professional services. Premium specifications, efficient layouts, and modern building services.

RERA Registered: The project is RERA registered (UPRERAPRJ184164, UPRERAPRJ893178), ensuring transparency and buyer protection.

Sector 140A offers strategic positioning on the Noida Expressway:

Emerging Commercial Hub: Sector 140A is developing as a premium commercial destination with multiple IT parks, commercial complexes, and corporate offices creating a thriving business ecosystem.

Noida Expressway: Direct connectivity to the 6-lane expressway ensures excellent accessibility to Delhi, Greater Noida, and the Yamuna Expressway corridor.

Metro Accessibility: The Noida Aqua Line metro runs parallel to the expressway with Sector 142 station nearby, providing efficient public transport for employees.

Corporate Ecosystem: Surrounded by established commercial developments including Logix City Centre, Stellar IT Park, and multiple IT parks, creating a critical mass of corporate activity.

Residential Catchment: Premium residential areas including Sectors 137, 143, 107, and Greater Noida West provide a large talent pool and daily commuters.

CRC The Flagship presents exceptional investment potential:

Multiple Income Streams: Investors can choose between serviced suites for regular rental income, offices for corporate leasing, or retail for high-visibility businesses—diversifying risk and optimizing returns.

Strong Rental Yields: Grade-A commercial properties in the expressway corridor typically yield 6-8% annually. Serviced suites, with professional management, often provide 6-7% yields.

Capital Appreciation: Commercial properties along the Noida Expressway have shown consistent 12-18% annual appreciation. Sector 140A's emergence as a commercial hub positions properties for sustained growth.

Corporate Tenant Demand: The expressway corridor's IT ecosystem creates strong demand for quality office spaces and serviced accommodation. IGBC Platinum certification attracts premium tenants, reducing vacancy risk.
