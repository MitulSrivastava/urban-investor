Project Overview

Welcome to Crown Residences at Godrej Golf Links, an exclusive residential development nestled within one of Greater Noida's most prestigious golf townships. Developed by Godrej Properties in partnership with ACE Group, this premium project offers thoughtfully designed 3 and 4 BHK residences that combine modern architecture, expansive layouts, and breathtaking golf course views. Spread across the iconic Godrej Golf Links township, Crown Residences is part of a 100-acre integrated golf community featuring landscaped gardens, wellness zones, clubhouse facilities, and a professionally designed 9-hole golf course.
