Deep Dive Investment Guide

Everything you need to know about Max 105

Max 105 by Max Estates represents a new paradigm in ultra-luxury living along the Noida Expressway. Developed by Max Estates, part of the diversified Max Group with interests spanning healthcare, insurance, and real estate, this project brings corporate sophistication and wellness-centric design to Sector 105, Noida.

What distinguishes Max 105 is its focus on low-density living with independent floors. Unlike conventional high-rise apartments, each floor is designed as a premium independent residence, offering the privacy and space of a villa with the security and amenities of a premium community.

The project's location in Sector 105 places residents at one of Noida Expressway's most prestigious addresses. With proximity to corporate hubs in Sectors 126, 142, and 144, excellent metro connectivity, and established infrastructure, Max 105 offers the perfect balance of accessibility and exclusivity.

Max 105 is designed around Max Estates' philosophy of 'wellness-integrated living.' Every aspect of the development—from building orientation to material selection—is crafted to enhance the physical and mental wellbeing of residents. The project features biophilic design elements that bring nature into daily living spaces.

The low-density configuration means fewer homes across the development, translating to more open spaces, better ventilation, and a sense of exclusivity. Each floor operates as an independent residence, with private elevators opening directly into the living area, ensuring complete privacy for families.

The wellness theme extends to the amenity spaces, which include meditation pavilions, reflexology paths, temperature-controlled pools, and dedicated wellness centers. The landscaping incorporates native species that purify air and create micro-climates, making outdoor spaces comfortable throughout the year.

Max 105 introduces an array of premium features designed for luxury and wellness:

  • Independent Floors: Premium low-rise floors (G+4) with private elevators, offering villa-like privacy with apartment convenience.
  • Ultra-Low Density: Limited homes per acre ensures maximum open spaces, privacy, and excellent air quality throughout the development.
  • Wellness Amenities: Temperature-controlled pool, spa, yoga pavilion, meditation gardens, and dedicated wellness centers.
  • Premium Specifications: Italian marble flooring, modular kitchens, premium bathroom fittings, and VRV air conditioning throughout.
  • Panoramic Views: Each floor is designed to maximize natural light and offer views of the Noida Expressway greens and surrounding developments.
  • Smart Home Integration: Comprehensive home automation for lighting, climate, security, and entertainment systems.

Max 105 offers premium independent floors designed for discerning families:

2 BHK Premium Floors: Spacious 2-bedroom independent floors starting from 1,200 sq.ft., ideal for young professionals, couples, or small families seeking luxury and privacy.

3 BHK Luxury Floors: Premium 3-bedroom independent floors ranging from 1,600-1,900 sq.ft., featuring spacious living areas, modular kitchens, and master suites with walk-in closets.

4 BHK Ultra-Luxury Floors: Expansive 4-bedroom independent floors from 2,000-2,400 sq.ft., offering dedicated family lounges, study areas, multiple balconies, and premium finishes throughout.

Private Terraces: Upper floors include private terraces with barbeque counters, pergolas, and seating areas, perfect for entertaining guests or quiet relaxation.

Sector 105 on the Noida Expressway is one of the most established and prestigious addresses:

Expressway Connectivity: Direct access to the 6-lane Noida-Greater Noida Expressway ensures seamless connectivity to Delhi, Greater Noida, and the Yamuna Expressway. The commute to South Delhi takes approximately 30-40 minutes.

Metro Connectivity: The Noida Aqua Line metro runs parallel to the expressway with stations at Sector 101 and Sector 104 nearby, providing efficient public transport for daily commuters.

Corporate Hubs: Major IT parks and corporate offices in Sectors 126, 127, 142, 144, and 135 are within a 10-15 minute drive, making it ideal for IT professionals and corporate executives.

Established Social Infrastructure: Premium schools including Amity International, Genesis Global, and Lotus Valley are nearby. Leading hospitals including Fortis, Jaypee, and Apollo are within 20 minutes.

Retail & Entertainment: Select Citywalk, DLF Mall of India, and various local markets provide extensive shopping, dining, and entertainment options within 15-20 minutes.

Max 105 presents a compelling investment opportunity in one of Noida's most premium locations:

Low-Density Premium: Independent floors in low-density developments command significant premiums due to scarcity. These configurations typically appreciate 15-20% faster than conventional apartments.

Corporate Rental Demand: Sector 105's proximity to IT hubs creates strong rental demand from senior executives and expats. Investors can expect 3.5-4.5% rental yields, significantly above market average.

Brand Assurance: Max Estates' association with the diversified Max Group ensures corporate governance, transparency, and quality. The developer's reputation adds a 10-15% premium in resale markets.

Infrastructure Growth: The upcoming Jewar Airport connectivity via the expressway, new metro extensions, and commercial development in surrounding sectors will drive sustained appreciation. Sector 105's established nature provides stability while offering growth potential.
