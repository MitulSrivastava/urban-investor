Top 10 Luxury Residential Projects in Noida (2026 Edition)

An institutional-grade analysis of Noida’s ultra-prime residential boom — square-foot appreciation, architectural specifications, and RERA credentials for discerning investors.

September 10, 2026 8 Min Read RERA Verified Data

Noida’s luxury residential landscape is undergoing its most profound structural expansion in two decades. While the wider NCR market has seen steady momentum, the expressway sectors—specifically Sector 150, Sector 151, and Sector 146—have emerged as the undisputed epicenters of institutional capital and ultra-high-net-worth individual (HNWI) acquisitions.

With the imminent commercial opening of the Noida International Airport at Jewar, the expansion of corporate headquarters along the expressway, and strict low-density master zoning regulations enforced by the Noida Authority, premium residential inventory is experiencing unprecedented demand. Below is our research team’s definitive, vetted guide to the Top 10 Luxury Residential Projects in Noida for 2026.

"In 2026, luxury real estate in Noida is no longer defined by superficial cosmetic marble. Discerning buyers now demand low-density tower layouts (less than 40 units per acre), acoustic glass envelopes, centralized VRV cooling, and comprehensive sports and wellness ecosystems."

1. Experion Saatori (Sector 151, Noida)

Developed by Experion Developers (backed by Singapore’s AT Capital Group), Experion Saatori has set a new benchmark for ultra-luxury residential living along the Noida-Greater Noida Expressway. Positioned in the tranquil, low-density pocket of Sector 151, this landmark development focuses on Japanese Zen philosophy, expansive biophilic design, and ultra-wide panoramic decks.

Experion Saatori

  • Location: Sector 151, Noida Expressway
  • Configurations: 3 & 4 BHK Ultra-Luxury Residences
  • Starting Price: From ₹3.60 Cr*
  • Key Highlights: 85% open greens, VRV AC, private lift lobbies
View Project Brochure & Floor Plans

2. ACE Arte & ACE Parkway (Sector 150, Noida)

ACE Group has played an instrumental role in turning Sector 150 into Noida’s most prestigious residential address. With ACE Arte and ACE Acreville, the group delivers art-deco master architecture, 51+ sports amenities, Olympic-grade recreational clubs, and direct connectivity to the Shaheed Bhagat Singh 42-acre park.

ACE Arte / Parkway

  • Location: Sector 150, Sports City
  • Configurations: 3 & 4 BHK Luxury Condominiums
  • Starting Price: On Request / Resale & Pre-Launch
  • Key Highlights: Adjacent to 42-acre city park, Art Deco clubhouse
Explore ACE Arte Details

3. Dasnac Yuva (Sector 146, Noida)

Located adjacent to the Sector 146 Metro Station on the Aqua Line, Dasnac Yuva represents the vanguard of sustainable, engineering-first residential developments. Backed by Dasnac’s proprietary ‘E-Homes’ construction technology and LEED Platinum environmental protocols, it appeals strongly to senior corporate executives working in Sector 135 and Sector 142 IT corridors.

4. Eldeco 7 Peaks (Sector 12, Central Noida)

While expressway sectors dominate new launches, Eldeco 7 Peaks is the premier luxury redevelopment in Central Noida. Offering unmatched proximity to Central Delhi, Sector 18 shopping district, and prime educational institutions, Eldeco provides established families an opportunity to upgrade without leaving Central Noida.

5. Gaur Chrysalis (Sector 22D, Yamuna Expressway)

For investors prioritizing exponential capital appreciation tethered to Jewar Airport and the upcoming Film City, Gaur Chrysalis and Gaur Alaris offer premier residential towers with unobstructed views of the YEIDA green belt and direct multi-lane transit corridors.

6. Crown Residences by Godrej Properties & ACE (Greater Noida)

Situated inside the landmark Godrej Golf Links township, Crown Residences features low-rise luxury golf apartments overlooking a masterfully manicured 9-hole golf course. Unrivaled serenity, private putting greens, and luxury concierge services make it an HNWI sanctuary.

7. CRC 150 (Sector 150, Noida)

Designed by renowned architect Hafeez Contractor, CRC 150 introduces double-height entrance lobbies, three-side-open apartment layouts, and zero vehicular movement on the surface podium. It offers one of the most balanced price-to-luxury ratios in Sector 150.

8. Sobha Rivana (Sector 1, Greater Noida West)

Sobha’s legendary German-standard backward integration ensures unmatched structural finishing at Sobha Rivana. With imported marble flooring, precision-engineered joinery, and acoustic fenestration, it is Noida West’s benchmark luxury offering.

9. Eldeco Ballads of Bliss & Whispers of Wonder

Eldeco’s resort-themed master communities—Eldeco Ballads of Bliss and Eldeco Whispers of Wonder—provide expansive water features, jogging tracks, and high-spec clubhouses tailored for luxury end-users seeking green tranquility.

10. Max 105 (Sector 105, Noida Expressway)

For buyers who desire intimate, boutique high-end residences with immediate expressway access, Max 105 delivers curated residences with low-density living, advanced security access, and bespoke hospitality management.
