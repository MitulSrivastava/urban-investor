Everything you need to know about Ace Parkway 2.0

Ace Parkway 2.0 is an ultra-luxurious residential project by Ace Group in Sector 150, Noida offering 3, 4 & 4.5 BHK apartments across 11 towers on 15 acres with approximately 790 units for exclusive low-density living.

Ace Parkway 2.0 is located in Sector 150, Noida — a premium residential sector along the Noida Expressway with excellent connectivity to Delhi, Greater Noida, and the upcoming Jewar International Airport.

Ace Parkway 2.0 offers ultra-luxurious 3 BHK (1900 sq.ft), 4 BHK (2600 sq.ft), and 4.5 BHK apartments designed for families seeking spacious, premium living.

With only 790 units across 15 acres, Ace Group's reputation, a coveted Sector 150 address, and BSP starting at ₹16,995/sq.ft, Ace Parkway 2.0 offers exceptional exclusivity and appreciation potential.
