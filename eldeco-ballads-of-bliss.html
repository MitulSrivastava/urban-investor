Deep Dive Investment Guide

Everything you need to know about Eldeco Ballads Of Bliss

Eldeco Ballads Of Bliss represents harmonious living in the heart of Noida. Developed by the Eldeco Group with over four decades of excellence in real estate, this project is designed for those who seek a blend of urban convenience and serene tranquility. Located in Sector 135, one of Noida's most prestigious sectors, the project offers residents the perfect balance between connectivity and calm.

The project's poetic name reflects its design philosophy—creating spaces that celebrate the poetry of daily life through thoughtful architecture, ample green spaces, and comprehensive amenities. Every aspect is designed to enhance residents' wellbeing and create a sense of contentment.

For homebuyers and investors, Ballads Of Bliss offers the Eldeco assurance of quality construction, transparent dealings, and strong after-sales service. The location in Sector 135 provides excellent connectivity to corporate hubs while maintaining a peaceful residential character.

Eldeco Ballads Of Bliss is conceived as a premium residential development that prioritizes peaceful living without compromising on connectivity and convenience. The project features well-designed towers surrounded by extensive landscaping, creating an oasis of calm in bustling Noida.

The architecture emphasizes natural ventilation and light, with units designed to maximize cross-ventilation and minimize dependence on artificial cooling. The towers are positioned to ensure optimal spacing, providing privacy and views for every residence.

The landscaping at Ballads Of Bliss is particularly noteworthy. Theme gardens, meditation zones, walking paths, and water features create multiple environments for relaxation and recreation. The central landscape serves as the heart of the community, where residents can connect with nature and each other.

World-class amenities including a clubhouse, swimming pool, sports facilities, and children's play areas ensure that every family member has access to recreational opportunities within the community.

Eldeco Ballads Of Bliss offers an array of premium features:

  • Prime Location: Located in Sector 135, Noida, offering excellent connectivity to corporate hubs and expressway while maintaining a peaceful residential character.
  • Eldeco Quality: Developed by Eldeco Group with 40+ years of track record, ensuring quality construction, timely delivery, and transparent dealings.
  • Extensive Landscaping: Theme gardens, meditation zones, water features, and walking paths creating a serene environment.
  • Premium Clubhouse: Well-appointed clubhouse with indoor games, multipurpose hall, and social spaces.
  • Sports Facilities: Swimming pool, gymnasium, badminton courts, and dedicated sports zones.
  • Security: Multi-tier security with CCTV surveillance, gated entry, and 24x7 security personnel.

Eldeco Ballads Of Bliss offers thoughtfully designed residential configurations:

2 BHK: Efficient 2-bedroom homes ideal for young professionals, small families, and those seeking quality living at accessible price points. These units feature well-designed layouts with maximum space utilization.

3 BHK Premium: Spacious 3-bedroom homes with large living areas, modular kitchen provisions, and master suites with attached bathrooms. These units are designed for comfortable family living.

3 BHK + Servant Room: Enhanced configurations with dedicated servant accommodations, ideal for families requiring household support.

4 BHK: Premium 4-bedroom residences for large families who value personal space, featuring multiple balconies, family lounges, and superior specifications.

Sector 135 offers an excellent location in established Noida:

Noida Expressway: Direct connectivity to the 6-lane Noida-Greater Noida Expressway, providing seamless access to Delhi, Greater Noida, and the Yamuna Expressway.

Corporate Hubs: Major IT parks and corporate offices in Sectors 125-144 are within 10-15 minutes, making this location ideal for IT professionals working along the expressway corridor.

Metro Connectivity: The Noida Aqua Line metro provides efficient public transport, with Sector 137 metro station accessible within 10 minutes.

Established Infrastructure: Sector 135 is a developed sector with operational social infrastructure including schools, hospitals, markets, and daily conveniences.

Educational Institutions: Several premium schools including Lotus Valley and Genesis Global are within 15 minutes, making this ideal for families with children.

Eldeco Ballads Of Bliss presents solid investment potential:

Established Location: Sector 135 is a developed location with stable appreciation. Properties in established sectors typically show lower volatility compared to emerging areas.

Corporate Rental Demand: Proximity to expressway IT parks creates strong rental demand from IT professionals. Investors can expect 3-4% annual rental yields.

Brand Premium: Eldeco Group's reputation ensures strong resale value and consistent demand, adding 10-15% premium over properties by lesser-known developers.

Infrastructure Catalyst: Metro connectivity enhancements and upcoming infrastructure including Jewar Airport link roads will enhance accessibility and property values in established expressway sectors.
