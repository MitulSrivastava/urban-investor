Everything you need to know about Grandthum

Grandthum is a landmark 23-acre commercial, retail, and entertainment project by Group 108 (promoted by A L Softweb Pvt Ltd) located in Techzone 4, Greater Noida West. It offers Grade-A office spaces, high-street retail, a 5-screen multiplex, and serviced studio apartments around a 2-acre artificial water body. Registered under UP RERA: UPRERAPRJ585534 (Phase I) and UPRERAPRJ213153/02/2026 (Phase III / Tower C).

Grandthum is located at Plot No. 7, Sector Techzone 4, Greater Noida West (Noida Extension), Uttar Pradesh 201308. It fronts a 130-meter wide road, situated approximately 5 minutes from Gaur Chowk and facing the proposed Aqua Line metro corridor.

Grandthum offers Grade-A commercial office spaces (lockable units and corporate floors), serviced studio apartments, high-street retail stores, hypermarket space, and multi-cuisine dining establishments.

Grandthum benefits from a strategic location in a high-density urban catchment of Greater Noida West, strong road connectivity, and upcoming metro infrastructure. With Grade-A office spaces, anchor retail, an IGBC Platinum pre-certified green campus, and independent quality monitoring by Tata Projects, it provides solid long-term commercial leasing and appreciation potential.

Deep Dive Investment Guide

Everything you need to know about Grandthum

When evaluating commercial real estate opportunities, selecting the right phase within a comprehensive development is key. Grandthum Phase III comprises Tower C, representing a major office and serviced studio offering within Group 108's 23-acre commercial development in Greater Noida West.

Phase III builds upon the development momentum of earlier project phases. It introduces a fresh wave of inventory that captures all the locational and footfall benefits of the integrated campus while offering new launch pricing options for buyers. The campus incorporates high-profile retail anchors, an operational hypermarket, and dining boulevards, creating an active destination for business and leisure.

Furthermore, Tower C integrates with the broader commercial ecosystem rather than operating as an isolated office block. The presence of high-street retail, multiplex entertainment, and food plazas supports corporate leasing appeal. For commercial buyers, this positions the asset in an established growth corridor backed by ongoing infrastructure development across Greater Noida West and high demand for Grade-A workspaces.

Grandthum, developed by Group 108 under promoter entity A L Softweb Pvt Ltd, stands as one of the largest mixed-use commercial developments in Greater Noida West. Spread across 23 acres, the project redefines modern workplace design by forging a truly integrated commercial ecosystem. It seamlessly intertwines world-class retail spaces, premium office towers, serviced studio apartments, and family entertainment facilities into a single destination.

The architecture of Grandthum is anchored by iconic Twin Towers scaling up to 34 storeys, connected by an expansive central plaza and a signature 2-acre artificial water body featuring dining yachts and musical fountains. Designed for entrepreneurs, corporate enterprises, and retail brands, the campus offers an environment where business and lifestyle coexist effortlessly.

The project delivers comprehensive business and leisure infrastructure: a hypermarket for daily conveniences, high-end retail storefronts, an expansive multi-cuisine food court, fine-dining restaurants, and a 5-screen multiplex. For professionals working in Tower C, this means unparalleled convenience—hosting client meetings at restaurants downstairs, networking in the central plaza, or entertaining visiting delegates seamlessly.

Strategic zoning ensures that the corporate office environment remains productive and tranquil, while the retail and entertainment zones generate healthy daily footfall. Grandthum is engineered as an IGBC Platinum pre-certified, energy-efficient campus with independent construction monitoring by Tata Projects.

For commercial buyers, the launch of Tower C Phase III represents an opportune entry into Greater Noida West's commercial market:

  • Launch-Stage Pricing: By entering during this phase, buyers secure initial pricing before rate revisions take effect as construction advances.
  • Inventory Selection: Buyers have the opportunity to choose preferred unit layouts and orientations—such as lake-facing offices or corner studio suites—subject to inventory availability.
  • Structured Payment Plans: Phase III offers developer payment plans linked to construction milestones, providing structured capital outlay options.
  • Long-Term Value Potential: As Noida Extension's commercial infrastructure matures and footfall builds across the Grandthum ecosystem, Tower C offers sustained leasing appeal for corporate and professional tenants.

The studio apartments at Grandthum are designed to cater to working professionals, consultants, and corporate visitors seeking furnished accommodations within an integrated business hub.

Corporate Leasing & Business Travelers: With multinational corporations and IT firms expanding across Noida and Greater Noida, demand for short-to-medium-term corporate housing continues to grow. These studio suites offer convenient, self-contained accommodations close to regional business hubs.

Professionals & Hybrid Work: Executives and consultants operating within Grandthum's office towers or nearby IT corridors find these studios well-suited as primary bases or weekday accommodations that eliminate long daily commutes.

Healthcare & Institutional Catchment: Proximity to major educational institutions, commercial centers, and healthcare facilities supports steady accommodation demand across various visitor profiles.

Flexible Leasing Models: Serviced studio units provide owners with diverse leasing options—including corporate tie-ups, executive leases, and long-term rentals—supported by round-the-clock campus amenities.

Grandthum offers meticulously planned office spaces designed to foster innovation, productivity, and corporate prestige. Spanning various configurations, these Grade-A workspaces are equipped to handle the demands of 21st-century businesses.

The professional ecosystem here is highly versatile, making it well suited for a diverse array of industries. IT and Tech Startups benefit from robust digital infrastructure, high power backup, and modern architecture. Consultants, Chartered Accountants, and Legal Firms find the corporate address and professional setting conducive to client engagements. Design, Architecture & Media Agencies can leverage inspiring panoramic views from upper floors.

Furthermore, floor plates are engineered to offer high efficiency, accommodating both boutique private offices and scalable multi-desk corporate headquarters. The surrounding retail, food courts, and transit corridors create an ideal working environment for team retention and recruitment.

Location is fundamental to commercial real estate success, and Grandthum occupies a prominent position in Greater Noida West (Noida Extension), offering connectivity and access to a massive residential population.

Gaur Chowk & Sector Techzone 4: The project is situated just minutes from Gaur Chowk along a 130-meter wide arterial expressway. It is surrounded by high-density residential developments, providing a captive retail footfall base and a deep talent pool for companies establishing offices here.

Transit & Road Networks (NH-24, FNG, Noida Sector 62): The site offers direct access to NH-24 (NH-9 Delhi-Meerut Expressway) and the upcoming FNG corridor. This connectivity ensures efficient commutes to Noida Sector 62, Electronic City, Greater Noida, and Ghaziabad.

Upcoming Infrastructure (Jewar Airport & Metro): The upcoming Noida International Airport at Jewar and the planned Aqua Line Metro extension toward Greater Noida West will further enhance regional transit links, bolstering commercial property demand over the coming years.

Investing in commercial assets requires evaluating macro infrastructure drivers, micro-market supply, and long-term tenant absorption. Grandthum Phase III represents a significant commercial addition in Greater Noida West.

Commercial Corridor Expansion: Greater Noida West has evolved from a predominantly residential cluster into a self-sustaining urban center. As hundreds of residential societies achieve full occupancy, the demand for organized Grade-A commercial offices, healthcare, and retail infrastructure has increased proportionally.

Occupier Appeal & Footfall Integration: Unlike standalone office complexes, mixed-use ecosystems that combine corporate workspaces with high-street retail, multiplexes, and dining generate sustained daily footfall. This integrated environment attracts reputable retail brands and corporate tenants seeking comprehensive lifestyle conveniences for their workforce.

Regional Connectivity & Infrastructure: Ongoing transit developments—including the expansion of the Noida Metro Aqua Line corridor, key arterial connectivity via NH-24 and FNG Expressway, and accessibility to the upcoming Noida International Airport at Jewar—continue to strengthen Greater Noida West's commercial viability.
