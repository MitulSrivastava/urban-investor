Everything you need to know about ONYX by Splendor

ONYX by Splendor represents Splendor Group's premium commercial offering in Sector 142, Noida—established as one of Noida's most sought-after business districts. Located at Plot 6, this development introduces a comprehensive mix of commercial offices, IT suites, and retail spaces designed for modern businesses.

Splendor Group has established a reputation for quality commercial developments across NCR, and ONYX reflects their commitment to creating efficient, modern workspaces that meet the evolving needs of businesses. The project is RERA registered (UPRERAPRJ920006/03/2024), ensuring transparency and buyer protection.

For investors and businesses, ONYX offers Grade-A commercial spaces in a location with established corporate ecosystem, excellent connectivity, and strong tenant demand. The combination of lockable offices, IT suites, and retail spaces allows investors to choose configurations matching their objectives.

ONYX by Splendor is designed as a comprehensive commercial destination catering to diverse business needs. The project offers three distinct product categories: commercial offices for professional service firms and corporate offices, IT suites for IT/ITES companies requiring efficient workspaces, and retail spaces for businesses requiring high-street presence.

The building's architecture emphasizes efficiency, natural light, and flexibility. Floor plates are designed to accommodate both smaller lockable units and larger open office spaces, allowing businesses of various sizes to find suitable configurations.

Modern building services including high-speed elevators, 100% power backup, air conditioning, and advanced security systems ensure seamless business operations. The project's infrastructure is designed for IT companies, with fiber-optic connectivity, adequate power, and modern HVAC systems.

The retail component faces the main road, providing excellent visibility and accessibility for businesses requiring footfall and frontage.

  • Prime Location: Plot 6, Sector 142—established commercial district with excellent visibility and accessibility on Noida Expressway.
  • Diverse Products: Commercial offices, IT suites, and retail spaces catering to varied business requirements.

  • Modern Infrastructure: High-speed elevators, 100% power backup, central air conditioning, fiber-optic connectivity.

Lockable Offices: Private, secure office spaces with independent locks, ideal for chartered accountants, lawyers, consultants, doctors, and professional service firms requiring dedicated spaces.

Various Sizes: Units available in multiple configurations ranging from compact offices to large floor plates, accommodating diverse business sizes and budgets.
