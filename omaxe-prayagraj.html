Everything you need to know about Omaxe Be Together Prayagraj

Omaxe Be Together Prayagraj, also known as Omaxe BeTogether Centre Point, represents a unique mixed-use commercial development in Civil Lines, Prayagraj—a city undergoing transformational infrastructure development. This project by Omaxe Group introduces an integrated concept combining retail shops, office spaces, food courts, hotel suites, and an integrated UPSRTC bus terminal.

The inclusion of an integrated bus terminal within the development is distinctive—this ensures continuous footfall from daily commuters and travelers, creating built-in customer traffic for retail businesses. Such public transport integration is rare and creates exceptional commercial value.

For investors and businesses, Omaxe Be Together offers the Omaxe Group's established reputation combined with a first-of-its-kind commercial format in Prayagraj. The project's RERA registration (UPRERAPRJ326163) ensures transparency and regulatory compliance.

Omaxe Be Together is designed as a comprehensive commercial ecosystem that combines multiple revenue-generating uses within one development. The concept integrates retail, office, hospitality, and transportation in a synergy that ensures consistent footfall and business viability.

The retail component features shops designed for various businesses—from flagship stores and showrooms to food & beverage outlets and service retail. The high-street positioning ensures maximum exposure and customer access.

Office spaces within the development serve professionals, businesses, and corporate offices seeking premium addresses in Civil Lines—the heart of Prayagraj's commercial and administrative district.

The hotel suites component provides accommodation options for business travelers, tourists, and families visiting Prayagraj—particularly significant given the city's importance as a religious and educational center. The food court and entertainment zones add dining and leisure options that enhance the destination's appeal.

Omaxe Be Together introduces unique commercial features:

  • Integrated Bus Terminal: UPSRTC bus terminal within the development ensures continuous footfall from commuters, creating built-in customer traffic for retail businesses.
  • Multiple Products: Retail shops, office spaces, food courts, and hotel suites creating a diversified commercial ecosystem.
  • Prime Location: Civil Lines, Prayagraj—the city's premier commercial and administrative district ensuring excellent accessibility and visibility.
  • Lifestyle Components: Food court, entertainment zones, and hotel accommodation creating a comprehensive destination experience.
  • Omaxe Quality: Developed by Omaxe Group with decades of experience in commercial real estate across North India.
  • RERA Registered: Project registered under RERA No. UPRERAPRJ326163 ensuring transparency and buyer protection.

Omaxe Be Together offers diverse commercial configurations:

Retail Shops: High-street retail units ranging from small shops to flagship stores, suitable for various retail formats including fashion, electronics, food & beverage, and services.

Office Spaces: Commercial offices designed for professional service firms, businesses, and corporate offices seeking premium addresses in Civil Lines.

Food Court Spaces: Units in the food court area, ideal for restaurants, cafes, food kiosks, and quick-service restaurants benefiting from high footfall.

Hotel Suites: Furnished accommodation units for short-term stays, managed professionally and generating rental income through hotel operations. Ideal for investors seeking passive income.

Civil Lines offers premium commercial positioning in Prayagraj:

Prime Commercial District: Civil Lines is Prayagraj's premier commercial and administrative district, housing major commercial establishments, administrative offices, and high-end retail.

Bus Terminal Footfall: The integrated UPSRTC bus terminal ensures continuous footfall from daily commuters and travelers—creating built-in customer traffic for retail and food businesses.

Connectivity: Excellent connectivity to all parts of Prayagraj and surrounding regions via road networks, making it easily accessible for customers and businesses.

Kumbh Mela Impact: Prayagraj hosts the Kumbh Mela—the world's largest religious gathering—ensuring global visibility and tourism potential that benefits commercial establishments.

University Presence: Allahabad University is nearby, creating demand from students, faculty, and visitors for retail, food, and accommodation services.

Omaxe Be Together presents unique investment potential:

Bus Terminal Advantage: The integrated bus terminal creates consistent footfall that few commercial developments can match. This built-in traffic ensures business viability even during economic fluctuations.

Diversified Returns: Investors can choose between retail, offices, food court units, or hotel suites—each with different risk-return profiles and management requirements.

Hotel Suite Yields: Professionally managed hotel suites generate regular rental income without requiring active investor involvement, ideal for passive investors.

Tier-2 City Growth: Prayagraj is undergoing infrastructure transformation with new development, improved connectivity, and economic growth. Properties in Civil Lines—the premium district—benefit from this overall city development.
