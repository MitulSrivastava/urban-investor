Deep Dive Investment Guide

Everything you need to know about Eldeco Echoes Of Eden

Eldeco Echoes Of Eden represents premium high-rise living in one of the Yamuna Expressway's most promising sectors—22D. Developed by Eldeco Group, a name synonymous with quality construction and timely delivery across North India for over four decades, this project introduces podium-style living with VRV air-conditioned homes.

The project's name reflects its design philosophy—creating living spaces that evoke the tranquility and beauty of an ideal garden paradise. The development features extensive podium-level amenities and gardens, ensuring that residents enjoy resort-style facilities without leaving the premises.

For homebuyers and investors, Echoes Of Eden offers the Eldeco assurance of quality, transparency, and value. The podium design maximizes open spaces, ensures efficient building services, and provides every unit with excellent ventilation and natural light.

Eldeco Echoes Of Eden is designed around the podium living concept, where amenities and gardens are placed on an elevated platform, creating a secure, traffic-free environment for recreation and relaxation. This design approach is increasingly popular in premium developments globally as it maximizes open spaces while ensuring safety.

The high-rise towers rise above the podium, ensuring that every unit enjoys views of the surrounding landscape, the podium gardens, or the Yamuna Expressway corridor. The towers are designed with optimal orientations to maximize natural light and cross-ventilation.

Every home at Echoes Of Eden comes equipped with VRV air conditioning—the premium climate control system that offers individual temperature control, energy efficiency, and superior comfort. This feature sets the project apart from conventional residential developments.

The podium level features themed gardens, swimming pool, club facilities, children's play areas, and recreational zones, creating a comprehensive lifestyle experience for residents.

Eldeco Echoes Of Eden introduces an array of premium features:

  • VRV Air Conditioning: Every home comes with VRV air conditioning, offering superior comfort, energy efficiency, and individual temperature control for each room.
  • Podium-Level Amenities: Elevated platform featuring swimming pool, gardens, club facilities, and recreational zones in a secure, vehicle-free environment.
  • Wraparound Balconies: Spacious balconies surrounding living areas, providing panoramic views and enhanced natural ventilation.
  • Low-Density Layout: Limited units per floor ensuring privacy, exclusivity, and efficient service delivery.
  • Penthouses: Exclusive penthouse units with private terraces, sky gardens, and panoramic views—offering the ultimate in luxury living.
  • Resort-Style Club: Premium clubhouse with indoor games, spa, gymnasium, party areas, and wellness facilities.

Eldeco Echoes Of Eden offers curated premium configurations:

3 BHK Premium: Spacious 3-bedroom homes with VRV air conditioning, modular kitchens, master suites with walk-in wardrobes, and wraparound balconies. Units range from 1,500-1,800 sq.ft.

3 BHK Ultra-Premium: Enhanced 3-bedroom configurations with additional balconies, premium finishes, and superior specifications throughout.

Penthouses: Exclusive duplex penthouses spanning 2,500+ sq.ft. with private terraces, sky gardens, and panoramic views. These units represent the pinnacle of luxury living at Echoes Of Eden.

All units feature: VRV air conditioning, premium flooring, modular kitchen provisions, fitted wardrobes, and smart home-ready infrastructure.
