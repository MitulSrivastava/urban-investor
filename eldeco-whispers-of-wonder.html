Project Overview

Eldeco Whispers of Wonder is a luxury residential development located in Sector 22D, Yamuna Expressway. Designed on the lines of the Radiant City concept by Le Corbusier, this premium 5-acre low-density community houses only 3 towers surrounded by stilt-level gardens. Each apartment is a corner unit featuring advanced VRV air-conditioning and spacious balconies. Conveniently located near the Noida International Airport and upcoming Film City, it represents a perfect blend of high-end lifestyle and exceptional connectivity.

Everything you need to know about Eldeco Whispers of Wonder

Eldeco Whispers of Wonder, often referred to as Eldeco WOW, represents Eldeco Group's premium offering in the fast-developing Yamuna Expressway corridor. Located in Sector 22D, this project introduces the sought-after combination of VRV air-conditioned homes with stilt-level gardens—a design innovation that maximizes green spaces and lifestyle amenities.

The project's evocative name reflects its promise: creating living spaces filled with wonder through thoughtful design, premium amenities, and a location poised for transformational growth. Every aspect of the development is designed to inspire delight and contentment.

For discerning homebuyers and investors, WOW offers the Eldeco Group's 40+ year legacy of quality, combined with cutting-edge features like VRV air conditioning, smart home infrastructure, and world-class clubhouse amenities. The project is RERA registered (UPRERAPRJ752382/09/2025), ensuring transparency and buyer protection.

Eldeco Whispers of Wonder is designed around the stilt-level development concept, where towers rise above a ground-level platform of gardens, amenities, and recreational spaces. This design maximizes open areas while ensuring that residents enjoy green views from every level.

The standout feature of WOW is its VRV (Variable Refrigerant Volume) air conditioning throughout all homes. This premium climate control system offers individual temperature control for each room, superior energy efficiency, and whisper-quiet operation—hence complementing the project's name.

The stilt level features landscaped gardens, themed courtyards, water features, and recreational zones. These spaces serve as extended living areas where residents can relax, exercise, or connect with neighbors. The design ensures that ground-floor units also enjoy elevated views due to raised plinths.

The world-class clubhouse is designed as a comprehensive lifestyle hub, featuring indoor sports, spa and wellness facilities, co-working spaces, party areas, and entertainment zones.

Eldeco Whispers of Wonder introduces an array of premium features:

  • VRV Air Conditioning: All homes equipped with premium VRV air conditioning offering individual temperature control, energy efficiency, and superior comfort.
  • Stilt-Level Gardens: Ground-level platform featuring themed gardens, courtyards, water features, and green spaces for all residents to enjoy.
  • 3 & 4 BHK Premium: Spacious configurations ranging from 1,600-2,500+ sq.ft. with thoughtfully designed layouts and premium specifications.
  • Low-Density Design: Limited units per floor ensuring privacy, efficient services, and an exclusive community experience.
  • World-Class Clubhouse: Comprehensive clubhouse with indoor sports, spa, gym, co-working spaces, and entertainment zones.
  • Smart Home Ready: Infrastructure for smart home features including automated lighting, climate control, and security systems.

Eldeco Whispers of Wonder offers premium VRV homes in thoughtful configurations:

3 BHK Premium: Spacious 3-bedroom homes with VRV air conditioning, large living areas, modular kitchens, and master suites with walk-in wardrobes. Units range from 1,600-1,800 sq.ft.

3 BHK Ultra-Premium: Enhanced 3-bedroom configurations with additional balconies, premium finishes, and superior specifications. Ideal for families seeking extra space and luxury.

4 BHK Luxury: Premium 4-bedroom residences ranging from 2,200-2,500+ sq.ft. featuring family lounges, study areas, multiple balconies, and the ultimate in spacious living.

All units feature: VRV air conditioning throughout, premium flooring, modular kitchen provisions, fitted wardrobes, smart home-ready infrastructure, and expansive balconies.

Sector 22D in Yamuna Expressway corridor offers exceptional positioning:

Noida International Airport (Jewar): Just 10-15 minutes from the upcoming international airport—North India's most transformative infrastructure project. Properties in this proximity are experiencing exceptional appreciation and demand.

Yamuna Expressway Connectivity: Direct access to the 6-lane expressway providing seamless connectivity to Noida, Greater Noida, Agra, and the entire NCR region.

Educational Hub: Major universities including Galgotias, Sharda, and Gautam Buddha University are within 15 minutes, creating demand from faculty, visiting professors, and families with students.

Film City: The proposed Film City near the airport will generate significant employment and economic activity, further driving property values in the corridor.

Commercial Development: IT parks, industrial zones, and commercial complexes are developing rapidly, creating a self-sustaining economic ecosystem.

Eldeco Whispers of Wonder presents exceptional investment potential:

VRV Premium: Homes with VRV air conditioning command premium rentals (15-20% higher) and resale values due to superior comfort and energy efficiency.

Airport Transformation: The Jewar Airport will fundamentally transform the Yamuna Expressway corridor. Properties within 15 minutes have historically shown exceptional appreciation in similar infrastructure projects globally—30-50% over 3-5 years.

Eldeco Brand Assurance: The Eldeco Group's 40+ year reputation ensures quality, timely delivery, and strong resale demand. Brand premium typically adds 10-15% to valuations.
