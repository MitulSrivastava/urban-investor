Deep Dive Investment Guide

Everything you need to know about Dasnac Yuva

Dasnac Yuva represents a LEED Platinum certified mixed-use development in Noida's Sector 124, brought to you by Dasnac Clearlake, a developer committed to sustainable and innovative construction. This project stands apart as one of the most environmentally conscious developments in the NCR region.

LEED Platinum certification represents the highest level of green building certification globally, ensuring the development meets stringent standards for energy efficiency, water conservation, indoor air quality, and sustainable materials. For residents, this translates into lower utility costs, healthier living environments, and the satisfaction of contributing to environmental sustainability.

The mixed-use format combines residential units with commercial spaces, creating a live-work-play ecosystem. This integrated approach allows residents to access offices, retail, and services within the development, reducing commute times and enhancing quality of life.

Dasnac Yuva is designed around the principles of sustainable development, achieving LEED Platinum certification through comprehensive green building strategies. The project incorporates energy-efficient systems, water conservation measures, waste management protocols, and sustainable materials throughout.

The residential component features apartments designed with optimal orientations for natural light and ventilation, reducing dependence on artificial lighting and cooling. Double-glazed windows, high-performance insulation, and energy-efficient appliances further enhance efficiency.

The commercial component provides Grade-A office spaces and retail units designed for modern businesses. These spaces appeal to companies committed to sustainability and employee wellbeing, ensuring strong tenant demand.

Beyond sustainability, the project features modern design, premium amenities, and comprehensive facilities. The integration of residential, commercial, and retail creates a self-sustaining ecosystem where residents have access to daily necessities within the development.

Dasnac Yuva introduces comprehensive sustainable features:

  • LEED Platinum Certification: Highest green building certification ensuring energy efficiency, water conservation, and sustainable construction.
  • Mixed-Use Development: Integration of residential, commercial, and retail creating a live-work-play ecosystem.
  • Energy Efficiency: Solar panels, LED lighting, high-performance insulation, and energy-efficient systems reducing utility costs.
  • Water Conservation: Rainwater harvesting, sewage treatment, water-efficient fixtures, and landscape irrigation using recycled water.
  • Premium Amenities: Clubhouse, fitness center, landscaped gardens, and recreational facilities for residents.
  • Excellent Connectivity: Location in Sector 124 provides expressway access and proximity to corporate hubs and metro stations.

Dasnac Yuva offers diverse configurations for residents and businesses:

Residential Apartments: Well-designed apartments in 2, 3, and 4 BHK configurations with energy-efficient features, quality finishes, and sustainable materials.

Office Spaces: Grade-A commercial spaces designed for IT/ITES companies, startups, and professional service firms. Units range from 500 sq.ft. to large floor plates.

Retail Units: Ground-floor retail spaces ideal for food & beverage, banks, pharmacies, and services catering to residents and office-goers.

All units feature: Energy-efficient systems, quality specifications, and sustainable materials aligned with LEED Platinum standards.

Sector 124 offers strategic positioning in Noida:

Noida Expressway: Direct connectivity to the 6-lane expressway providing seamless access to Delhi, Greater Noida, and beyond. Sector 124 is positioned along the expressway corridor.

Metro Connectivity: The Noida Aqua Line metro provides efficient public transport with multiple stations nearby, enhancing connectivity for employees and residents.

Corporate Ecosystem: Surrounded by IT parks and corporate offices in Sectors 125-127, creating strong demand for both residential and commercial spaces.

Established Infrastructure: The area benefits from developed social infrastructure including schools, hospitals, markets, and entertainment zones in adjacent sectors.

Future Connectivity: The FNG Expressway and other infrastructure projects will further enhance connectivity to Haryana and Ghaziabad.

Dasnac Yuva presents compelling investment potential:

Green Premium: LEED Platinum certified buildings command 15-20% rental premiums and attract premium tenants—corporations committed to sustainability. They also maintain higher occupancy rates.

Mixed-Use Advantage: Integrated developments create self-sustaining ecosystems that attract diverse tenants and customers, reducing vacancy risk and maximizing returns.

Lower Operating Costs: Green buildings typically have 30-40% lower energy and water costs, enhancing net returns for investors and reducing total cost of ownership for buyers.

Corporate Rental Demand: Location in the expressway corridor creates strong demand from IT professionals and companies seeking quality residential and commercial spaces, yielding 6-8% for commercial and 3-4% for residential.
