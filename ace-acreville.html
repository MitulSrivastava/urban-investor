Deep Dive Investment Guide

Everything you need to know about Ace Acreville

Ace Acreville represents Ace Group's flagship integrated township on the Yamuna Expressway. Spread across 100 acres, this neo-classical themed development introduces a lifestyle proposition that combines architectural grandeur with future-ready connectivity and comprehensive amenities.

The project's defining feature is its scale and vision—as a 100-acre integrated township, Ace Acreville creates a self-sustaining community with residential plots, developed homes, commercial spaces, educational facilities, and extensive open areas. This township approach ensures that residents have access to all necessities within the community.

For investors and homebuyers, Ace Acreville offers the Ace Group's established reputation for quality delivery combined with the growth potential of the Yamuna Expressway corridor. The township's location in Sector 22A places it minutes from the upcoming Jewar Airport, positioning it at the heart of the region's transformation.

Ace Acreville is designed as a comprehensive integrated township where every aspect of daily life is addressed within the community. The neo-classical architectural theme features grand arches, fluted columns, symmetrical facades, and classical proportions—creating a sense of timeless elegance throughout the development.

The township offers multiple residential options including plotted developments where homeowners can construct customized homes, villas and independent floors, and premium apartments. This variety ensures that buyers can choose configurations that match their lifestyle preferences and budgets.

Beyond residential, the township includes commercial zones for retail and services, educational institutions, healthcare facilities, and extensive parks and recreational areas. The master plan ensures that residents have access to schools, hospitals, markets, and entertainment within the township itself.

The extensive infrastructure development includes wide internal roads, underground utilities, rainwater harvesting, sewage treatment plants, and extensive landscaping—creating a sustainable, well-planned community.

Ace Acreville introduces comprehensive township features:

  • 100-Acre Scale: Expansive integrated township with residential, commercial, educational, and recreational zones within one community.
  • Neo-Classical Design: Architectural theme featuring grand arches, fluted columns, and classical proportions creating timeless elegance.
  • Multiple Options: Residential plots for custom homes, villas, independent floors, and premium apartments catering to diverse preferences.
  • Comprehensive Amenities: Clubhouse, swimming pool, sports facilities, parks, themed gardens, and recreational zones.
  • Township Infrastructure: Wide roads, underground utilities, sewage treatment, rainwater harvesting, and 24x7 security.
  • Social Facilities: Schools, healthcare centers, retail zones, and community facilities planned within the township.

Ace Acreville offers diverse residential configurations:

Residential Plots: Premium plots in various sizes (200-500 sq.m.) where homeowners can construct customized homes according to their preferences. Plots offer maximum flexibility and customization potential.

Villas: Ready-to-move or under-construction independent villas with private gardens, parking, and premium specifications. Villas offer the ultimate in privacy and exclusivity.

Independent Floors: G+3 or G+4 format floors offering villa-like privacy with community amenities. Each floor operates as an independent residence with dedicated parking.

Premium Apartments: Well-designed apartments in towers with clubhouse access and comprehensive amenities. These offer community living with extensive shared facilities.

Sector 22A on the Yamuna Expressway offers strategic positioning:

Noida International Airport (Jewar): Just 10-15 minutes from the upcoming international airport—the region's most transformative infrastructure. Properties in townships near the airport are experiencing exceptional demand and appreciation.

Yamuna Expressway: Direct connectivity to the 6-lane expressway providing seamless access to Noida, Agra, and the broader NCR region. The expressway is being widened to enhance capacity.

Educational Institutions: Major universities including Galgotias, Sharda, and Gautam Buddha University are within 15-20 minutes. The area is emerging as an educational hub attracting families.

Film City: The proposed Film City near the airport will generate thousands of jobs and significant economic activity, benefiting nearby townships.

Commercial Development: IT parks, industrial zones, and logistics hubs are developing rapidly along the expressway, creating employment and economic growth.

Ace Acreville presents exceptional investment potential:

Scale Advantage: As a 100-acre township, Ace Acreville benefits from economies of scale and comprehensive infrastructure. Integrated townships typically appreciate faster than standalone projects due to self-sustaining ecosystems.

Airport Proximity: Properties within 15 minutes of major airports have historically shown exceptional appreciation globally. The Jewar Airport will transform this corridor, driving 40-60% appreciation over 5 years.

Plot Investment: Plots offer maximum flexibility, customization potential, and historically higher appreciation compared to built properties. Plot values in developing corridors typically double every 4-5 years.

Rental Income: Once developed, properties in integrated townships command premium rentals due to comprehensive amenities and self-sustaining infrastructure. Investors can expect 3-4% yields.
