Project Overview

Fairfox EON (Eye of Noida) is an innovative mixed-use commercial destination spread across 13.65 acres in Sector 140A, Noida. It integrates premium IT/ITES office spaces and high-street retail zones shaped by inspirational architecture. Built as a Grade 'A' development, the project is IGBC Gold certification compliant, featuring an elevated skywalk network, multiple plazas, and a 42-meter-wide green avenue lined with F&B outlets. It offers unmatched connectivity, situated within walking distance of the metro station and only 20 minutes from the upcoming Jewar International Airport.
