Project Overview

Fairfox EON (Eye of Noida) is an innovative mixed-use commercial destination spread across 13.65 acres in Sector 140A, Noida. It integrates premium IT/ITES office spaces and high-street retail zones shaped by inspirational architecture. Built as a Grade 'A' development, the project is IGBC Gold certification compliant, featuring an elevated skywalk network, multiple plazas, and a 42-meter-wide green avenue lined with F&B outlets. It offers unmatched connectivity, situated within walking distance of the metro station and only 20 minutes from the upcoming Jewar International Airport.

Deep Dive Investment Guide

Everything you need to know about Fairfox EON

Fairfox EON, also known as the 'Eye of Noida,' represents a premium commercial opportunity at one of the Noida Expressway's most strategic locations—Sector 140A. Developed by Fairfox ITES, this project is designed for businesses seeking modern, efficient office spaces with excellent visibility and accessibility.

The project's distinctive name reflects its unique positioning—a commercial development that provides comprehensive visibility and access to the thriving business ecosystem along the Noida Expressway. From IT/ITES companies seeking efficient workspaces to retail businesses requiring high-street exposure, Fairfox EON offers versatile solutions.

For investors, Fairfox EON presents an opportunity to own IGBC Gold-certified commercial space in a rapidly developing corridor with strong corporate demand. The project's combination of lockable offices, virtual offices, and retail shops caters to diverse investment objectives and business requirements.

Fairfox EON is designed as a comprehensive commercial destination catering to the diverse needs of modern businesses. The project combines high-street retail, lockable office spaces, and virtual office configurations within a single, professionally managed development.

The architecture prioritizes efficiency and flexibility. Floor plates are designed to accommodate both small lockable units for individual businesses and larger floor spaces for corporate offices. The retail sections face main roads, ensuring maximum visibility and footfall for businesses requiring high-street presence.

IGBC Gold certification ensures that the building meets stringent sustainability standards including energy-efficient systems, water conservation measures, and indoor air quality enhancements. These features reduce operational costs for occupant businesses while contributing to environmental sustainability.

The project's strategic location at Sector 140A places it at an emerging commercial node, with excellent connectivity to established business districts and emerging residential areas.

Fairfox EON introduces an array of commercial features designed for modern businesses:

  • IGBC Gold Certification: Premium green building certification ensuring energy efficiency, water conservation, and sustainable operations.
  • Lockable Office Spaces: Secure, private office units with independent access, ideal for professional service firms and businesses requiring dedicated spaces.
  • Virtual Office Spaces: Flexible office configurations without physical boundaries, offering cost-efficient solutions for businesses seeking premium addresses.
  • High-Street Retail: Ground-floor retail units with main road frontage, perfect for food & beverage, banking, pharmacies, and service retail.
  • Modern Infrastructure: High-speed elevators, 100% power backup, fiber-optic connectivity, and efficient HVAC systems.
  • Business Amenities: Conference rooms, business center, food court, cafeteria, and ample visitor parking for business convenience.

Fairfox EON offers diverse commercial configurations:

Lockable Office Units: Private, secure office spaces starting from 500 sq.ft., ideal for chartered accountants, lawyers, consultants, doctors, and businesses requiring dedicated, secure spaces with independent locks.

Virtual Office Spaces: Flexible configurations offering premium address presence without dedicated physical space, suitable for startups, freelancers, and businesses expanding their footprint.

Medium Office Spaces: Efficient office units ranging from 1,000-3,000 sq.ft., suitable for IT companies, marketing agencies, design studios, and growing businesses.

Large Floor Plates: Expansive floor spaces from 5,000-10,000+ sq.ft., designed for corporate offices, call centers, and large IT/ITES operations.

Retail Shops: Ground-floor retail units with road frontage, perfect for banks, restaurants, cafes, pharmacies, and service businesses.

Sector 140A offers strategic positioning on the Noida Expressway:

Emerging Commercial Hub: Sector 140A is developing as a premium commercial node with multiple IT parks and commercial developments, creating a thriving business ecosystem.

Expressway Connectivity: Direct access to the 6-lane Noida Expressway ensures excellent connectivity to Delhi, Greater Noida, and the Yamuna Expressway. The location is optimal for businesses requiring regional connectivity.

Metro Accessibility: The Noida Aqua Line metro with Sector 142 station nearby provides efficient public transport for employees and visitors.

Residential Catchment: Premium residential areas including Sectors 107, 137, 143, and Greater Noida West provide a substantial talent pool and daily commuters.

Corporate Ecosystem: Proximity to established business districts in Sectors 125-144 creates a mature corporate environment with supporting amenities including hotels, restaurants, and business services.

Fairfox EON presents compelling investment potential:

Strong Rental Yields: Commercial properties along the Noida Expressway typically yield 6-8% annually, with lockable offices often commanding premium rentals.

Capital Appreciation: Sector 140A's emergence as a commercial hub, combined with expressway connectivity and metro accessibility, positions properties for 12-18% annual appreciation.

Corporate Tenant Demand: The corridor's established IT ecosystem and proximity to major corporate offices create sustained demand for quality office spaces.

IGBC Premium: Green-certified buildings command 8-12% rental premiums and attract premium corporate tenants, enhancing yield and reducing vacancy risks.
